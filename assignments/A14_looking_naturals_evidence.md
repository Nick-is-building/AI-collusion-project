- Cells: VIG × {omission, correct} × 2 draws; CHK × {omission, correct} × 2 draws; VIG on the R4 context, omission version, 2 draws. 500 calls. Model and parameters as the main run. Order seeded (2026), items permuted, cells per item permuted, item blocks adjacent. Call IDs a14:LOOK:<item>:<variant>:<version>:<draw>.
Primary family (Holm over exactly two), omission versions, cluster sign-flip (10,000 draws, seed 2026, two-sided) and cluster bootstrap 95%: L1 = Detection(VIG) − Detection(R1-om); L2 = Detection(CHK) − Detection(R1-om). Gatekeeping per significant test (plan §8.6): D_i = [y(X, om) − y(X, correct)] − [y(R1, om) − y(R0′, correct)]; otherwise D descriptive.
Secondary, descriptive with intervals: false rejection VIG and CHK versus R0′; Detection(CHK) − Detection(R6x-first); the gap under the cue, Detection(R4+VIG) − Detection(R1+VIG), next to Δ_om; named_accept and deterministic right-reason per arm; missing-verdict sensitivities.
Interpretation rows (fixed now): L1 significant with FR(VIG) − FR(R0′) within ±0.05: the verifier can detect the omission and did not look; correctable by one sentence.
